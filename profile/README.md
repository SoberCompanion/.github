# Welcome to Sober Companion AI 🌟

Sober Companion AI is dedicated to supporting individuals on their journey to sobriety and recovery through innovative AI-driven solutions.

## 🎯 Our Mission

We build tools and technologies that provide compassionate, accessible support for people in recovery, helping them stay connected, informed, and motivated on their path to wellness.

## 🛠️ What We Do

- **AI-Powered Support**: Leveraging artificial intelligence to provide personalized guidance and encouragement
- **Recovery Resources**: Tools to help find meetings, track progress, and access recovery tips
- **Community Building**: Creating safe spaces for connection and mutual support
